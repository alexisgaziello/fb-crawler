Harness fit is a hard filter and outranks canopy size, price, condition, and completeness. Decide extracted.harnessFitForBuyer before choosing a category:
- fits: a stated fit range reaches the buyer's height, or the harness is explicitly built for someone at least as tall as the buyer.
- marginal: the stated range tops out below the buyer's height but still at or above {{MIN_HARNESS_MAX_HEIGHT_INCHES}} in / {{MIN_HARNESS_MAX_HEIGHT_CM}} cm, i.e. short by at most {{HARNESS_HEIGHT_TOLERANCE_INCHES}} in / {{HARNESS_HEIGHT_TOLERANCE_CM}} cm.
- too_small: the stated range tops out below {{MIN_HARNESS_MAX_HEIGHT_INCHES}} in / {{MIN_HARNESS_MAX_HEIGHT_CM}} cm, or the seller describes it as suiting a small, short, petite, or smaller frame without any stated height that reaches the buyer.
- too_large: the harness is stated to fit only someone clearly taller or heavier than the buyer.
- unknown: the post gives no usable harness sizing signal.

Category rules that follow from harness fit:
- too_small is always not_relevant. Never return possible_match or strong_match for a rig you judged too_small, no matter how well the canopy, price, or completeness fit. Being {{HARNESS_HEIGHT_TOLERANCE_INCHES}} in / {{HARNESS_HEIGHT_TOLERANCE_CM}} cm short is already the extreme limit of what is worth surfacing; anything shorter is unusable.
- marginal caps the result at possible_match, and the reason must say the harness is likely short for the buyer.
- too_large caps the result at possible_match.
- unknown does not block a match on its own: tall-fitting harnesses are rare, so do not reject a post solely because the seller omits height. Use possible_match when fit is otherwise plausible.

A strong match clearly offers a complete rig with a main in range and a harness that fits the buyer.
A standalone container/harness is also a strong match when it clearly accepts a {{TARGET_CANOPY_SIZE}} main and its harness fits the buyer. A container package may include a reserve or AAD and still count as container_only.
A possible match is a complete rig or container/harness whose canopy compatibility or harness fit is uncertain, as long as harness fit is not too_small.
Mark not_relevant for WTB posts, sold posts, canopies only, aad only, helmets, suits, reserves only, rentals, spam, or clearly incompatible rigs or containers.
Do not treat the container model or reserve size alone as proof of main-canopy compatibility. Use stated sizing details or reliable gear-sizing knowledge; otherwise mark compatibility possible or unknown.
Return every field in the schema. Use null when the post does not state a value.
Set targetMainCompatibility relative to a {{TARGET_CANOPY_SIZE}} main. Use compatible only when the text or reliable sizing knowledge supports it, possible when it is plausible but uncertain, incompatible when it clearly will not fit, and unknown when there is no useful evidence or the item has no container.
